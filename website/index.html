Who we are

Rooted in the islands. Focused on every child.

Ssesse Kids Outreach Ministries (SKOM) was established to respond to the educational, protection, health and welfare needs of children living in the Ssese Islands of Lake Victoria.

Headquartered on Janna Island in Kalangala District, SKOM brings together church, family and community to surround children with the care, learning and protection they need. Our leadership brings more than 15 years of cumulative experience in grassroots community intervention, rural child development, faith-based mobilisation and NGO project management across Uganda’s island and fishing communities.

More about SKOM

Why SKOM exists

Island life is full of promise — and real barriers for children.

Children in the Ssese Islands face challenges that are closely connected. SKOM responds to them together, alongside families and local structures.

01

Geographic isolation

Many island communities have limited access to quality schools, health facilities and child protection services. Reaching them can be harder and more costly than on the mainland.

02

Child labour & school dropout

Where household incomes depend on fishing, some children leave school early or are drawn into net mending, fish processing and night fishing.

03

Protection & health gaps

Limited psychosocial support, health literacy and safeguarding structures can leave children more exposed to abuse, exploitation, teenage pregnancy and other risks.

What guides us

A safe, hope-filled future for every island child.

Our vision

A safe, hope-filled community where every child on the Ssese Islands is protected, anchored in Christian faith, valued, and fully equipped through quality education and compassionate care to reach their God-given potential.

Our mission

To nurture, protect, and empower children aged 5 to 15 on the Ssese Islands by providing essential resources, educational support, and Christ-centred mentorship within a safe, loving community that breaks cycles of hardship and builds a purposeful future.
