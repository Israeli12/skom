Why support children in the Ssese Islands

Full of potential. Too often out of reach.

The Ssese Islands are home to children with the same dreams and gifts as children anywhere. But island life can place quality schools, health services and child protection out of reach.

Where families depend on fishing, some children leave school early or take on work such as net mending, fish processing and night fishing. Limited psychosocial support and safeguarding structures can leave children more vulnerable.

SKOM exists to change that — with learning, care, protection and community working together.

Geographic isolation

Services are harder and costlier to reach on the islands.

Child labour & dropout

Fishing economies can pull children out of school.

Protection & health gaps

Limited support and safeguarding structures increase risk.
