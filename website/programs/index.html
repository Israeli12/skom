Our programs

Four pillars working together for the whole child.

A child who is learning also needs to be healthy, safe and surrounded by a supportive family and community. SKOM’s four program pillars are designed to work as one.

Learning+ Well-being+ Protection+ Community
01 · Learning & Skills 02 · Well-being 03 · Child Protection 04 · Family & Community
Pillar 01

Holistic Learning & Skill Development

Education and practical skills give children the confidence and tools to build a stronger future.

  • Establish 10 community learning hubs
  • Provide scholastic materials
  • After-school tutoring and mentorship
  • Life-skills development
  • Vocational exposure for children aged 12–15
Explore Learning & Skills
Pillar 02

Physical & Emotional Well-being

Children need more than education. They need safe environments where they can grow physically, emotionally, socially and spiritually.

  • Health and hygiene education
  • Nutrition support
  • Psychosocial support groups
  • Safe recreation
  • Sports programs
Explore Well-being
Pillar 03

Child Safeguarding, Equity & Inclusion

Every child has the right to be safe. Child protection is a priority across everything SKOM does.

  • Develop and implement a Child Protection Policy
  • Train community child protection teams
  • Advocate against child labour
  • Work with Beach Management Units (BMUs)
  • Promote inclusion and child safety
Explore Child Protection
Pillar 04

Family & Community Engagement

SKOM does not work with children in isolation. Families, churches, young people, community leaders and local structures are part of the solution.

  • Parenting workshops through churches
  • Youth community service
  • Lake conservation activities
  • Monitoring, Evaluation & Learning
  • Community participation
Explore Family & Community

How it fits together

One program, four connected pillars.

SKOM’s 2026–2029 program brings the four pillars together with the aim of reaching 3,000+ children across the Ssese Islands.
