Pillar 01 · Holistic Learning & Skill Development

Learning that opens a stronger future.

Education and practical skills help children stay in school, grow in confidence and imagine a future beyond the limits of their circumstances. SKOM’s learning pillar brings that support closer to home.

What this pillar includes Education partnerships

Why it matters

Keeping children learning in island communities.

On the islands, distance, cost and the pull of fishing work can interrupt a child’s education.

SKOM’s learning pillar aims to make learning support more accessible — through community-based hubs, practical materials, tutoring and mentorship — while also helping children develop the life skills, leadership and confidence they need.

For older children aged 12–15, vocational exposure offers an early look at practical skills and future pathways.

What this pillar includes

Planned learning & skills activities.

These activities form part of SKOM’s 2026–2029 program.

Community learning hubs

SKOM seeks to establish 10 community learning hubs across the islands — places where children can study, read and receive support within their own communities.

Scholastic materials

Providing the books, stationery and learning materials that help children take part fully in school.

After-school tutoring

Additional academic support, tutoring and mentorship to help children keep up, catch up and grow.

Life skills

Nurturing critical thinking, emotional regulation and communication — skills children carry into every part of life.

Leadership & confidence

Helping children develop leadership, autonomy and the confidence to use their voice.

Vocational exposure (ages 12–15)

Introducing older children to practical skills and potential career pathways as they approach adolescence.

Strategic objectives

What this pillar sets out to do.

As agreed by SKOM’s founding members.

  • Nurture life skills: critical thinking, emotional regulation and communication
  • Promote leadership, autonomy and confidence
  • Support academic growth through tutoring, mentorship and skills training

Education partnerships

Help bring learning closer to island children.

SKOM welcomes partners who share a commitment to education for children in the Ssese Islands. Areas for partnership include:

Learning hubs

Support the establishment and running of a community learning hub.

Materials

Contribute scholastic materials, books and learning resources.

Tutoring & mentorship

Help resource after-school tutoring and mentorship.

Skills exposure

Share practical skills and career insight with older children.
