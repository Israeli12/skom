Pillar 04 · Family & Community Engagement

No child grows up alone. Neither does lasting change.

SKOM does not work with children in isolation. Families, churches, young people, community leaders and local structures are all part of the solution.

What this pillar includes

A circle of care

Everyone around a child has a part to play.

Families

Parents and caregivers supported with resources, workshops and updates.

Churches

Local churches as trusted places for gathering, teaching and parenting support.

Young people

Children and youth serving their communities and caring for the lake.

Community leaders

Local leaders and structures taking part in decisions and feedback.

What this pillar includes

Planned family & community activities.

These activities form part of SKOM’s 2026–2029 program.

Parenting workshops through churches

Working through local churches to equip parents and caregivers to nurture and protect their children.

Youth community service

Involving children and young people in serving their own communities.

Lake conservation

Environmental stewardship activities that help children care for Lake Victoria and the islands.

Community participation

Creating space for families and community members to shape and take part in SKOM’s work.

Monitoring, Evaluation & Learning

Measuring impact through program evaluation and community feedback — and learning from it.

Keeping caregivers informed

Partnering with caregivers through resources and regular updates.

Strategic objectives

Strong families. Engaged communities. A cared-for lake.

  • Partner with caregivers through resources, workshops and updates
  • Involve children in community service and environmental stewardship
  • Measure impact through program evaluation and community feedback

As agreed by SKOM’s founding members.

Churches and communities: walk with us.

SKOM welcomes churches, fellowships and community groups who want to support families and children in the Ssese Islands.
