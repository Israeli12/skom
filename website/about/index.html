About SKOM

Community-rooted. Christ-centred. Child-focused.

Ssesse Kids Outreach Ministries (SKOM) exists so that children on the Ssese Islands can learn, grow, stay safe and reach their God-given potential — surrounded by families, churches and communities that care for them.

Meet our leadership Organisational status

Who we are

A local response to the needs of island children.

SKOM is a Christ-centred, community-based organisation established to address the educational, protection, health and welfare needs of children living in the Ssese Islands of Lake Victoria.

We are a Non-Governmental Organisation aligned to the NGO Act, 2016, headquartered on Janna Island in Kalangala District. Our work focuses on children aged 5 to 15, and on the families, churches and community structures around them.

SKOM’s leadership brings more than 15 years of cumulative experience in grassroots community intervention, rural child development, faith-based mobilisation and NGO project management across Uganda’s island and fishing communities.

Our story

From a shared conviction to a clear plan.

SKOM was founded by Pastor Bamanya John, its Founder and Chairperson, together with Co-Founder Ganyana Caroline. The founding members came together around a shared conviction: that every child on the islands deserves a real chance to learn, to be safe and to flourish.

Guided by the Chairperson, the founding members drafted and adopted SKOM’s vision and mission, and agreed that the organisation’s work would be guided by four core pillars — learning, well-being, safeguarding and community engagement.

Those pillars now shape SKOM’s 2026–2029 strategic partnership and resource mobilisation program, which aims to reach 3,000+ children across the Ssese Islands.

Vision, mission & motto

What we are working towards.

Vision

A safe, hope-filled community where every child on the Ssese Islands is protected, anchored in Christian faith, valued, and fully equipped through quality education and compassionate care to reach their God-given potential.

Mission

To nurture, protect, and empower children aged 5 to 15 on the Ssese Islands by providing essential resources, educational support, and Christ-centred mentorship within a safe, loving community that breaks cycles of hardship and builds a purposeful future.

SKOM’s vision and mission were adopted by its founding members and will be reviewed and refined following the organisation’s pilot study.

Where we work

The Ssese Islands of Lake Victoria.

The Ssese Islands archipelago lies in Kalangala District, Central Uganda. Many of its communities depend on fishing, and many are reached only by water.

Who we serve

SKOM’s primary beneficiaries are children aged 5–15 living in the Ssese Islands. Because children thrive within strong families and communities, our work also reaches the parents, caregivers, churches, young people and community leaders who surround them.

Why SKOM exists

Connected challenges need a connected response.

Geographic isolation

Limited access to quality schools, health facilities and child protection services — and costly, difficult logistics for delivering them.

Child labour & dropout

Economic dependence on fishing contributes to school dropout and exposes some children to net mending, fish processing and night fishing.

Protection & health gaps

Limited psychosocial support, health literacy and safeguarding structures can increase children’s vulnerability to harm.
