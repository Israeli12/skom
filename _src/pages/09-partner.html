Partnership opportunities

Ways to partner.

Each partnership is shaped together. Below are some of the ways organisations and individuals can work with SKOM.

[[icon globe]]

Development agencies & foundations

Partner on the 2026–2029 program as a whole, or on a specific pillar.

  • Program funding for one or more pillars
  • Technical collaboration on child protection or education
  • Joint Monitoring, Evaluation & Learning
[[icon building]]

Corporate partners

Connect your organisation’s social commitment to children in island communities.

  • Sponsor a community learning hub
  • Provide scholastic or in-kind materials
  • Offer vocational exposure for children aged 12–15
[[icon church]]

Churches & faith-based institutions

Walk alongside a Christ-centred ministry serving island children and families.

  • Support parenting workshops through local churches
  • Mission and fellowship partnerships
  • Prayer and mobilisation within your congregation
[[icon users]]

Community partners

Local leaders, Beach Management Units and community structures are central to SKOM’s work.

  • Child protection and child labour prevention
  • Community participation and feedback
  • Youth service and lake conservation

Resource mobilisation

Where partnership can make a difference.

Resources mobilised for the 2026–2029 program can contribute to:

See the full strategy [[icon arrow]]
Learning & Skills
Community learning hubs (10 planned), scholastic materials, after-school tutoring, life skills and vocational exposure for children aged 12–15.
Physical & Emotional Well-being
Health and hygiene education, nutrition support, psychosocial support groups, safe recreation and sports programs.
Child Safeguarding, Equity & Inclusion
Developing and implementing a Child Protection Policy, training community child protection teams, advocacy against child labour and work with Beach Management Units.
Family & Community Engagement
Parenting workshops through churches, youth community service, lake conservation, community participation and Monitoring, Evaluation & Learning.
