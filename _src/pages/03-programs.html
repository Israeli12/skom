Four pillars working together for the whole child.

A child who is learning also needs to be healthy, safe and surrounded by a supportive family and community. SKOM’s four program pillars are designed to work as one.

Child Safeguarding, Equity & Inclusion

Every child has the right to be safe. Child protection is a priority across everything SKOM does.

  • Develop and implement a Child Protection Policy
  • Train community child protection teams
  • Advocate against child labour
  • Work with Beach Management Units (BMUs)
  • Promote inclusion and child safety
